# FlexFold Product Requirements Document

## [PRD_OVERVIEW] Product overview
FlexFold is an integrated laundry handling system for space-constrained urban homes. It replaces a static basket with a unit that stores, sorts and transports laundry and folds flat when not in use. It realises Concept 1, "The Integrated System", which won the concept selection matrix with a weighted score of 4.25 against 3.65 for the hygiene-only hard shell. Scope of this PRD: the launch SKUs FlexFold Lite, FlexFold, FlexFold Heritage Edition and FlexFold Pro, plus the three accessories.

## [PRD_USERS] Target users and jobs to be done
Primary: Anjali, a dual-income 2BHK household manager doing 4-5 loads a week, whose basket handle has already failed. Secondary: hostel and PG students carrying laundry down stairs to a shared machine. Tertiary: senior households who find lifting difficult. B2B: co-living operators and hostels buying in bulk. Functional jobs: hold dirty clothes without overflow, move a full load in one trip with a hand free, sort once, keep the corner free of odour. Emotional job: feel the home is under control without extra mental load.

## [PRD_REQUIREMENTS] Functional requirements
FR-1 Movement: two-wheel tilt-and-roll base with a telescoping handle; must climb a 20 mm threshold and roll on tile, marble and stairs edges. FR-2 Sorting: three removable compartments of about 22 litres each (66 litres total), colour-coded, each with its own carry handles. FR-3 Hygiene: ventilated side panels plus an antimicrobial-treated, machine-washable liner; charcoal insert pocket. FR-4 Space: folds to 8 cm depth in under 10 seconds without tools. FR-5 Durability: reinforced handle-to-body joint. FR-6 Repairability: wheels and liner replaceable by the user. FR-7 Lite variant: no wheels and no telescoping handle, two compartments, folds flat. FR-8 Care hub: QR code on the product opens warranty registration, a capacity guide and accessory reorder.

## [PRD_NFR] Durability tests, materials and warranty
Handle and joint: 15 kg static load for 24 hours and 10,000 lift cycles at 8 kg without failure. Wheels: 5 km rolling test at 8 kg load. Fold mechanism: 5,000 fold cycles. Liner: 50 wash cycles at 40 degrees C with antimicrobial efficacy retained (ISO 20743 test to be commissioned). Weight: FlexFold under 2.4 kg, Lite under 1.2 kg. Materials: 600D recycled polyester shell, powder-coated steel frame, TPR wheels. Warranty: three years on frame, wheels and handle; one year on liner. A ten-year warranty, as offered by Brabantia on steel bins, is not credible for a fabric product and is not offered.

## [PRD_KANO] Feature prioritisation (Kano)
Must-be: reinforced handle and joint, so durability is table stakes rather than a differentiator. Performance: tilt-and-roll wheels and the three compartments. Attractive (delighters): fold-flat 8 cm and the ventilated antimicrobial liner. Polarising: the heritage sleeve is indifferent to about half of respondents and a delighter to over a third, which is why it is sold as a separate Heritage Edition SKU rather than the default pack. Indifferent: weight-sensor app and UV-C dock, deferred to the Year-2+ roadmap. Classes come from the Phase-0 Kano survey (n=600).

## [PRD_ROADMAP] Release roadmap
Release 1 (launch): FlexFold, FlexFold Lite, Heritage Edition with two sleeves, Liner Set, Charcoal Insert, Wheel Kit, QR care hub. Release 2 (months 7-18): five heritage sleeves, FlexFold Pro bulk packaging, colour variants. Release 3 (Year 2+): detachable delicates bag, family pack, drying-rack companion, weight-sensor pilot if Kano reclassifies it. Explicitly rejected: over-door sling, under-bed drawer, backpack carrier, UV-C dock at launch.

## [PRD_METRICS] Product success metrics
Average rating at least 4.3 across launch SKUs within four months. Warranty claim rate under 3% in Year 1. Return rate under 5% on D2C. Accessory attach rate at least 55% of consumer units in Year 1. Lapse rate (no accessory, referral or care-hub activity in months 7-12) under 30%.
