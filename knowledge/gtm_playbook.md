# Neat Nest Go-to-Market Playbook

## [GTM_PRICING] Price ladder and pricing architecture
Good-better-best ladder, all MRPs GST-inclusive and kept at or below Rs 2,500 so that the 5% GST rate on textile made-ups applies (18% above Rs 2,500): FlexFold Lite Rs 899, FlexFold Rs 1,799 (hero), FlexFold Heritage Edition Rs 2,299, FlexFold Pro Rs 1,399 for 50+ units. Accessories: Liner Set Rs 399, Charcoal Odour Insert 3-pack Rs 299, Wheel Kit Rs 249. The Rs 499 penetration price in the early business model canvas is withdrawn because it was below the realistic unit cost and contradicted the durability positioning.

## [GTM_ENTRY] Market entry phases
Phase 0 (Jan-Mar 2027): validate with a Van Westendorp and Kano survey (n=600) and a 50-unit beta with Mumbai and Pune housing societies. Phase 1 (Apr-Sep 2027): D2C and Amazon in Mumbai and Pune, two heritage sleeves, one co-living B2B pilot. Phase 2 (Oct 2027-Sep 2028): Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Delhi NCR, Flipkart, all five sleeves, accessories on quick commerce. Phase 3 (Years 2-3): selective Home Centre shop-in-shop and institutional contracts. Channels deliberately avoided: D-Mart, Meesho and deep-discount sale events.

## [GTM_RACE] RACE digital funnel
Reach: SEO against problem searches such as "laundry basket for small apartment", "basket that doesn't smell" and "wheeled laundry basket India"; short-video "overflow to solved" demos; creator partnerships with home-organisation creators; paid social on new-mover signals; housing-society activations. Act: landing page with the four-dimension comparison; the "Will it fit?" space quiz that answers fit anxiety. Convert: total-cost-of-ownership framing, warranty proof in retargeting, founding-batch urgency. Engage: WhatsApp care flow, accessory reminders, renter-to-renter referral, review request at 4-6 weeks.

## [GTM_TARGETING] Propensity-based targeting rule
Score every launch-campaign prospect with the propensity model. Spend retargeting and creator-code budget on the top three deciles first, which hold most buyers. Prospects who completed the "Will it fit?" quiz, have bought home-organisation products before, or score high on digital engagement have significantly higher odds of buying. Income and age alone are not reliable targeting signals.

## [GTM_RETENTION] Retention playbook by segment
Nest Advocates: referral rewards and early access to new sleeves. Steady Replenishers: liner and insert replenishment reminders timed to the wash cycle. Quiet One-timers: care-hub onboarding and a 60-day "how is it rolling?" WhatsApp check. Service-scarred: priority callback within 24 hours, a free wheel kit or liner when a product issue is logged, and root-cause review of the ticket. Rank customers by lapse risk and contact the riskiest 30% first. Validate the save rate with a holdout before scaling.

## [GTM_B2B] Institutional channel
Target co-living operators, college hostels and serviced apartments. FlexFold Pro at Rs 1,399 for 50+ units, with white-glove delivery and a three-year warranty. Use institutional reviews as social proof for the student segment.
